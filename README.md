**Entry:** "The office fire drill is the only meeting where everyone shows up on time, because it is the only one with an actual exit."

**Characters:** 122 (gate: 1–200, one line)

## Why it fits the brief

1. **On subject:** a joke about office life that lands as a joke.
2. **True to the office:** the fire drill is the one gathering where nobody is late — the alarm does not negotiate.
3. **Surprising turn:** the last words reframe every other meeting as a room with no way out — "the only one with an actual exit".
4. **Tight:** one line, no wasted setup, reads cleanly aloud in one breath.
5. **Original:** not a stock line — no "this meeting could have been an email", no printer gag.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.
